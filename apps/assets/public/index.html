Before you export

Three rules that keep us on brand

Dark green on brand greenAny text or icon on the green (#22C55E) is dark green #07301A — never white. Buttons, badges, stickers, lower-thirds.
No invented colors or gradientsUse only the palette in the style guide. The block colors mean something in the app (green = lesson, rose = exam) — never reshuffle them.
Claim only what's trueOne exam per term, zero sessions lost on rebalance, and per-tier daily AI quotas are guaranteed. Anything beyond that needs sign-off first.
