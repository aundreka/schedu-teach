Notes for the shoot

  • Actual button labels differ slightly from the script. The empty home says Create your first plan (not "Create my first plan"), steps advance with Continue, and the final button is Generate plan (not "Proceed"). Either update the VO or keep it loose; just do not caption the wrong label over the button.
  • The flow includes creating the subject first. Adding a subject means typing or snapping a syllabus, which the app turns into chapters and lessons before you reach the plan funnel. Budget for it in the edit; it speeds up nicely.
  • Reference footage exists. Videos has a real 5× capture of the entire flow (speedrun-zero-to-term.mp4), useful as a timing reference or as b-roll if a segment fails on set.
  • Record clean. Fresh account or the empty test account, notifications off, battery full or hidden, and hold the calendar reveal for a beat at 0:23; that is the payoff.
  • Type and color. Montserrat, green #00bf63, black, off-white #f9f9f9. If you add your own captions, the style guide has the caption bank and export specs.
