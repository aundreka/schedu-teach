Brand basics: colors and type

Click a swatch to copy its hex. These come straight from constants/colors.ts; don't invent tints or darken the green for contrast; use the pairings shown.

Core palette

Session-type ramp (calendar meaning, never decorative)

The one rule people get wrong: text and icons on the brand green are the dark green #07301A, never white; white on this green fails contrast. Every badge and button in the app follows this; your thumbnails should too.

Fast rules

Heavy, tight headlinesWeight 800, letter-spacing −0.02em, sentence case. The green goes on one word or phrase, not the whole line.
Tabular numbers everywhereCounters, quotas, and timers use tabular numerals so digits never jitter between frames.
White ground, soft grey stageCompositions sit on white; the demo area sits on #F4F6F8 with a hairline border and 20 px radius.
No white text on greenUse #07301A on any green fill: buttons, badges, stickers, lower-thirds.
No gradients, no drop-shadow textThe brand is flat soft-fills with colored borders. Depth comes from one soft shadow under cards, nothing else.
Don't recolor the rampLesson is green, exam is rose, and so on. The colors mean something in the app. Never swap them for looks.

For static creators

Post layouts

Three fixed layouts cover every placement. The dashed line is the 6% safe margin; nothing but background crosses it. The frame zone holds a scene end-frame (use the End frame button above and screenshot at 2×). Headline ink on white; one green phrase max; CTA pill is always green with dark-green text.
